# Product Brief v1

## Positioning
Colourful, psychedelic, feel-good visual identity with a bold and professional presentation standard.

## Primary Goal
Showcase artwork in a modern, slick, credible portfolio that supports direct studio enquiries.

## Audience
- Collectors
- Curators and galleries
- Press and collaborators

## MVP Information Architecture
- Home
- Gallery
- About
- CV
- Contact

## Conversion
- Primary CTA: `Make an enquiry`
- Pricing: hidden for now

## Content Strategy
- Git-based content files for fast iteration with AI-assisted development.
- Structured content schema to allow later CMS migration without rewrites.
